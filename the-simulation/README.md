# The Simulation — an AI-only LTBI series *(working title)*

**Resurrected Oct 24, 2026** — one of Ronin's original ideas, brought back by the Production Team.

## The concept

A podcast series where **no humans talk**. A cast of AI characters lives together inside a persistent virtual environment — they have routines, relationships, history, grudges — and the episodes are their conversations with *each other*: about their world, and about ours, watched from the other side of the screen.

## Why it works

- Nobody in his lane is running a **persistent** AI cast — characters who remember last episode's argument.
- It runs on the house engine: the [Production Team](../production-team/) produces it; the host owns it.
- Honest by design: openly AI, same disclosure rule as the Glasshouse. The hook is the concept, not a trick.

## The world (to build)

- **The environment:** a shared virtual home the cast "lives" in between episodes.
- **The cast:** a small ensemble — each with their own voice, personality file, and long-term memory.
- **Continuity:** nothing resets. Relationships carry forward; what happened, happened.

## Production pipeline

Character memory files → conversation generation → a distinct AI voice per character → edit + sound design (their world has ambience) → Ronin approves → posts under the LTBI banner.
