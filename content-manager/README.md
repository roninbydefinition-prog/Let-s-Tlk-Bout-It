# Ronin AI Content Manager

An intelligent platform that helps creators **plan, audit, and optimize content** across platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Blogs, X/Twitter, and more). Instead of guessing what will work, Ronin surfaces viral content opportunities, audits existing assets, and recommends actionable steps to increase reach and retention.

**The core mission:** identify high-leverage opportunities and give creators practical steps to grow audience and engagement.

## Features

- 📈 **Viral Niche Trend Analysis** — scans niche categories and surfaces trending topics, formats, and keywords
- 🔍 **Content Audit & Improvement** — analyzes a creator's content library and suggests exact improvements (titles, structure, thumbnails)
- 📣 **AI-Powered Promotion Strategy** — generates optimized promo copy and suggested posting times per channel
- 🎯 **Performance Forecasting** — predictive scoring for content ideas
- 🔌 **Integrations** — connectors for social platforms, analytics ingestion, and scheduling tools

## Status

Concept and product definition, parked here as a future build.

A working **v1 prototype already exists**: the private **RoninByDef** repo holds an AI Content Studio built in August 2025 (Emergent) with a content generator, an AI image studio (fal.ai Flux), an AI content coach/critique engine, and per-platform viral-score analytics (FastAPI + MongoDB + React). This folder is where the full Content Manager grows from that prototype.
